Objections, answered

Questions before
you book the call.

The questions most people ask before bringing on an outbound sales professional. Answered straight, the same way I'd handle them on a call.

How do I know you will bring in qualified buyers, not just calls?+

Every prospect is qualified through needs-based questions first. At Tunsam Autos I qualified leads against vehicle and financing criteria, so the sales team only spoke with high-intent buyers.

I am not in banking or automobiles. Can you work with my offer?+

The mechanics carry over: reach the right person, understand their need, overcome the objection, and advance the deal. I learn your offer fully before I speak to any of your prospects.

What happens when a prospect says they need to think about it?+

I ask what specifically they are unsure about. Vague hesitation usually hides one concrete question. I then re-engage by phone, WhatsApp Business, email, or social channels to keep the deal moving.

How do you handle objections?+

Through consultative selling: I understand what a customer actually needs before presenting a solution, then address the objection against that need instead of pushing harder.

What tools do you work with?+

HubSpot, Zoho, Airtable, Calendly, Make.com, Asana, WhatsApp Business, Google Sheets, and the Microsoft Office Suite.

Are you available for remote work?+

Yes. I am based in Ile-Ife, Osun State, and open to remote roles. I have worked remotely and independently, hitting daily output targets without direct supervision.

Appointment Setter, Tunsam Autos

Automobile Aug 2024 to Feb 2025 Nigeria

Tunsam Autos is a Nigerian automobile dealership providing vehicle sales and car financing to retail buyers. The role was to advance high-intent buyers into sales conversations.

Quick facts

  • Role Appointment Setter
  • Call volume ~200 / week
  • Tracking Calendly + Asana
  • Channels Phone, WhatsApp, email

The situation

Leads came from cold prospecting and advertising campaigns, and only some were ready to buy. The sales team needed the high-intent ones, not every one.

What I did

  • Drove about 200 outbound calls per week to cold prospects and inbound leads generated through advertising campaigns.
  • Qualified prospects against vehicle and financing criteria through structured needs questioning, advancing high-intent leads to the sales team.
  • Secured discovery and sales appointments with qualified buyers, coordinating bookings through Calendly to keep the pipeline moving.
  • Overcame objections on live calls and re-engaged non-converting prospects by phone, WhatsApp Business, email, and social channels, tracking performance in Google Sheets and Asana.

Why it mattered

A conversation only matters if the buyer can act on it. Qualifying first kept the pipeline moving with real opportunities.
